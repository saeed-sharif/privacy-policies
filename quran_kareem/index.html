Quran Kareem: Read & Pray

Official Privacy Policy

Package: com.kodnest.qurankareem • Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Quran Kareem: Read & Pray ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by KodNest. We are deeply committed to providing an authentic, clean, and distraction-free Quran reading and Islamic utility experience for Muslims worldwide.

Our Core Privacy Promise: We respect your privacy. This application operates with a privacy-first approach and does not require registration, personal user accounts, or tracking of your personal identity.

2. Information We Collect & Local Data Storage

We do not collect or harvest Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

  • Local Device DataStore: Your reading progress (last read page, Surah name), custom saved bookmarks, recent reading history, and Tasbeeh counter values are saved 100% locally on your device using Android DataStore.
  • No Remote User Profiling: None of your bookmarks or reading habits are uploaded to remote servers or shared with third parties.

3. App Permissions & Usage

To deliver essential Islamic features, the application requests the following device permissions:

  • Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used exclusively on your device to calculate accurate local Prayer Times and the exact Qibla Direction towards Mecca. Location coordinates are processed locally and are never stored or transmitted to external servers.
  • Internet Permission (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely to download the 13-line Quran PDF pages on-demand for offline viewing.

4. Third-Party Services & Hosting

The app connects to online endpoints strictly for downloading necessary Quran PDF assets:

  • Asset Downloads: High-quality Quran pages are fetched from Google Drive public storage endpoints. No personal telemetry or advertising identifiers are passed during these asset requests.

5. Data Retention & User Control

Since all data (bookmarks, reading history, tasbeeh counts) is stored locally on your device, you have total control over your data. You can erase all app data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is family-friendly and safe for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age, and the app contains no age-restricted or harmful content.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app enhancements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
